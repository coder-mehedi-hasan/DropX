# DropX imagery and illustration brief

## Photography

- Show real merchant, rider, parcel, and receiver moments in Bangladesh.
- Prefer purposeful movement and visible context over posed smiles.
- Use natural light, deep neutral environments, and one orange accent.
- Keep the subject large enough to crop for 16:9, 1:1, and 9:16 placements.
- Avoid generic trucks, anonymous warehouse scenes, excessive lens flare, and glossy stock-photo treatment.

## Illustration

- Use simple geometric forms, rounded corners, and 1.75px outline strokes.
- Base illustrations on Obsidian, Carbon, White, and Volt Orange.
- Use Terminal Green and Alert Amber only for semantic states.
- Do not create a second mascot, symbol, or competing delivery mark.

## Accessibility

- Provide meaningful alt text for editorial imagery.
- Keep text outside the image when possible so it can be translated and resized.
- Do not place body copy over detailed photography without a contrast-safe overlay.
