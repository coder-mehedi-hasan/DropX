Color in Practice

Color should make the next action obvious—not make people decode the interface.

Recommended ratio

60 / 30 / 10

60% Obsidian

Canvas, navigation, large surfaces.

30% Carbon

Cards, fields, secondary layers.

10% Volt

Actions, highlights, progress.

Accessibility rule

Never use color alone.

Pair status colors with a label, icon, or shape. Keep body text at 4.5:1 contrast and large text at 3:1 or better.

03b. Light & Dark Mode

One DropX identity, two carefully balanced environments.

☾

Dark mode

The primary DropX environment for operations, tracking, and rider workflows. Use Obsidian as the canvas and Carbon for raised surfaces.

Canvas#0D0F12
Surface#1A1D24

Light mode

Use for customer-facing pages, printable documents, and bright environments. Keep orange for action and progress—not for large backgrounds.

Canvas#F7F8FA
Surface#FFFFFF
Mode rule: do not simply invert colors. Preserve semantic meaning, contrast, elevation, and focus visibility when switching themes.

04b. Brand Voice

Every message should sound capable, direct, and human.

→

Direct

Lead with what happened and what to do next. Prefer “Pickup confirmed” over “Your pickup request has been successfully processed.”

◌

Reassuring

Be calm when plans change. Explain the next step, give a useful time frame, and avoid blame or vague promises.

✦

Energetic

Use momentum, not hype. Keep exclamation marks rare and let fast, useful experiences prove the promise.
